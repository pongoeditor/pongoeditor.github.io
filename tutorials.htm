How to Cut a Video with Pongo Video Cutter

Follow these simple steps to cut, trim, and split your video files using Pongo Video Cutter. The entire process takes less than 5 minutes.

Step 1: Open a Video File

Launch Pongo Video Cutter and click the "Open" button. Select the video file you want to cut from your computer. The tool supports MP4, AVI, MKV, and other popular formats.

Step 2: Set the Start and End Points

Use the timeline slider or enter specific timestamps to define the start and end of your clip. You can preview the selection to make sure it's correct before cutting.

Step 3: Choose Output Format & Destination

Select the desired output format (e.g., MP4, AVI) and choose a folder on your computer where the new video file will be saved.

Step 4: Cut and Save Your Video

Click the "Cut" or "Save" button. The tool will process the video. This is usually very fast because it uses "stream copy" technology without re-encoding, preserving the original quality.

Pro Tip: For best results, use the preview feature to ensure your cut points are exactly where you want them before saving.
