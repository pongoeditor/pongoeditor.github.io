Pongo Video Cutter - Tutorials

Pongo Video Cutter is a simple tool that lets you cut out parts of a video you don't want and keep only the parts you like. You can either:

  • Save all the good parts as one combined video, or
  • Save them as separate video files.

You don't need to know anything technical. Just load a video, mark the parts you want, and let the program do the rest.

Before You Start

Make sure:

  1. You have a video file on your computer.
    Supported formats: MP4, AVI, MKV, MOV, FLV, WMV, TS, M4V, WebM.

  2. The program has access to FFmpeg, a helper tool that does the actual cutting.
    If FFmpeg is missing, the program will tell you and show you where to get it.

How to Use Pongo Video Cutter – Step by Step

1. Open Your Video

You can do this in two ways:

  • Drag and drop your video file directly onto the program window.
  • Or click the Browse button and select your video.

Once the video is loaded, you'll see its details (filename, size, resolution, and duration) on the side panel.

2. Watch and Preview the Video

  • The video starts playing automatically after it's loaded.
  • Press the Spacebar anytime to Play or Pause.
  • Use the timeline slider below the video to jump to any moment.
  • If you have a very long video, use the Zoom In and Zoom Out buttons to see the timeline more clearly.

3. Mark the Parts You Want to Keep

This is the most important step. You'll create "segments," which are the parts of the video you want to keep.

  • Move the video to the moment where you want the segment to start.
  • Click "Set Start".
  • Move the video to the moment where you want that segment to end.
  • Click "Set End".

That's it. You've just created one segment. Repeat these steps to add more segments if you want.

  • You can see all your segments in the list on the side.
  • Click on a segment in the list to preview it.
  • If you make a mistake, select the segment and click "Remove Segment".
  • To delete all segments at once, click "Clear Segments".

4. Choose How You Want to Save

There are two options:

  1. Separate Video: Each segment will be saved as its own video file.
  2. Combined Video: All segments will be combined into a single video file.

To choose merging, tick the checkbox "Combined Video".

5. Export/Cut the Video

When you're ready:

  • Click the "Export" button.
  • A confirmation message will appear. Review the details.
  • Click "Yes" to start.

The program will now process the video. A progress bar will show you how far along it is. When it's finished, a message will appear, and the output folder will open automatically so you can see the result.

Quick Tips

  • If you're not sure where to start, remember:
    Set Start = beginning of the part you want.
    Set End = end of the part you want.
  • You don't need to cut perfectly. The program keeps everything between those two points.
  • You can always add, remove, or clear segments before cutting. Nothing is final until you click "Export".
