Lossless Video Cutting, Made Simple.

Pongo Video Cutter is a free and simple video cutting tool that lets you trim unwanted parts from your videos without losing quality. No re-encoding. No waiting. Just pure, lossless cuts - made simple for everyone.

Unlike traditional video editors that re-render your entire video and can reduce quality, Pongo Video Cutter uses lossless cutting technology. This means your output video keeps the exact same quality as the original file. Every detail stays sharp. Every frame stays perfect.

The program is lightweight, easy to use, and completely free. Just load your video, mark the parts you want to keep, and let Pongo do the rest in seconds.

Key Features

1. Lossless Cutting
Cut your videos without re-encoding. The output quality is 100% identical to the original video.

2. Super Fast Processing
Because there's no rendering, cutting videos only takes seconds — even for large files.

3. Simple & Intuitive Interface
No complicated settings. No confusing menus. Just load, mark, and cut.

4. Multi-Segment Cutting
Select multiple parts of a video and keep them all in one go.

5. Merge or Split
Choose to combine all selected segments into one video, or save them as separate files.

6. Drag & Drop Support
Simply drag your video file into the program window and start cutting.

7. Built-In Preview Player
Watch your video before cutting. Use the spacebar to play or pause anytime.

8. Zoomable Timeline
Zoom in and out on the timeline to make precise cuts, even on long videos.

9. No Quality Degradation
Your videos stay sharp and clear. Every output file matches the original quality exactly.

10. Free to Use
Pongo Video Cutter is completely free. If you love it, you can support the project with a donation.
