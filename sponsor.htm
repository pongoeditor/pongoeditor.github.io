Our Sponsors

This page is dedicated to all the amazing people who have supported Pongo Video Cutter through their generous donations. Your contribution helps keep this tool free and, more importantly, 80% of every donation goes directly to orangutan conservation and protection through:

  • BOSF — Borneo Orangutan Survival Foundation
  • COP — Centre for Orangutan Protection
  • YIARI — Yayasan Inisiasi Alam Rehabilitasi Indonesia
  • SOCP — Sumatran Orangutan Conservation Programme

Thank you for helping protect orangutans and their habitat. Every donation, no matter how small, makes a real difference.
