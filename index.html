Pongo Hard Drive
A lightweight storage diagnostic tool for Windows.

About

PHD (Pongo Hard Drive) is a portable command-line utility that reads drive health information directly from storage hardware. It reports device identity, health status, temperature readings, and the complete set of S.M.A.R.T. attributes for any internal or external drive connected to your system.

PHD runs entirely from a single executable. No installation is required. All report generation, diagnostics, and uploads happen locally on your machine.

Features

  • Device scan — Detect all connected storage devices automatically
  • Health report — Overall condition with clear pass/fail indication
  • Temperature — Current reading with historical low and high
  • S.M.A.R.T. table — All attributes with normalized values and raw counters
  • Self-test — Launch quick or deep diagnostic probes
  • Export — Save reports as TXT, JSON, or HTML
  • Publish — Share a report on this website with a single command
